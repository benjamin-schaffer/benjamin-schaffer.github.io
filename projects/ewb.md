# Engineers Without Borders – Local Project (Holyoke, MA)

**Organization:** Engineers Without Borders (UMass Amherst)  
**Community Partner:** Nuestras Raíces, Holyoke, MA  
**Role:** Chapter Representative & Fundraising Lead  
**Timeline:** Feb 2025 – Present  
**Focus Areas:** Renewable energy systems, structural assessment, community-based engineering

---

## Project Overview

The UMass Amherst Engineers Without Borders (EWB) Local Project partners with **Nuestras Raíces**, a nonprofit organization supporting Holyoke’s Puerto Rican and Latinx agricultural community. The primary objective is to **design and implement a low-cost biogas heating system** to sustainably heat a community greenhouse, extending the growing season while reducing reliance on fossil fuels.

In parallel, the project includes **structural assessment and repair** of an existing greenhouse damaged by weather exposure.

This work combines renewable energy engineering, structural evaluation, and community collaboration in a real-world, resource-constrained environment.

---

## Engineering Problem

Nuestras Raíces operates community greenhouses that face two key challenges:

- High winter heating costs and limited growing seasons
- Structural degradation from long-term weather exposure

The engineering challenge is to deliver a **reliable, maintainable, and affordable solution** that:
- Converts organic farm waste into usable heat energy
- Operates at small scale
- Can be maintained locally with minimal technical overhead
- Respects cultural, economic, and operational constraints of the community

---

## Technical Approach

### Biogas Heating System
- Conduct **feasibility studies** and system modeling for small-scale biogas production
- Evaluate organic waste inputs, heat output requirements, and system efficiency
- Develop prototype concepts for a **low-cost, replicable biogas heating unit**
- Prioritize simple construction, locally available materials, and ease of maintenance

### Structural Assessment
- Perform visual and structural assessments of an existing greenhouse
- Identify areas requiring reinforcement or repair due to weather damage
- Support planning for structural remediation alongside energy system integration

Engineering work is conducted through **weekly team meetings**, with guidance from experienced faculty advisors and iterative design reviews.

## Impact & Outcomes

### Projected Impact
- Reduced heating costs for community greenhouses
- Lower greenhouse gas emissions through renewable energy use
- Improved year-round crop yields and food security
- Educational opportunities for youth and community members in sustainable energy

### Long-Term Vision
- Create a **replicable model** for small-scale agricultural biogas systems
- Enable local ownership through documentation, training, and workshops
- Support continued collaboration between EWB and Nuestras Raíces beyond student involvement

The project aims to balance **technical rigor with human-centered design**, ensuring solutions remain functional, culturally inclusive, and sustainable over time.
