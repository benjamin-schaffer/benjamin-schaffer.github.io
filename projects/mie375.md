## Overview
This project was part of a semester-long manufacturing enterprise focused on the **design, validation, and production planning** of a PCB-mount assembly for a medical imaging device. The objective was to deliver a **tool-less, repeatable, and manufacturable assembly** capable of supporting **low-volume production at scale**.

I served as **Project Manager** for a **40-member enterprise** organized into **eight Business Units (BUs)**, responsible for coordinating requirements, schedules, design reviews, and cross-team communication.

---

## Project Objectives
- Design a PCB-mount assembly compatible with an existing baseplate
- Securely locate and retain a PCB and display mounting plate
- Enable **tool-less installation and removal**
- Design all components for **manufacturability and repeatability**
- Demonstrate feasibility for **~5,000 assemblies per year**

---

## Enterprise Structure & Leadership
The manufacturing enterprise was divided into **8 Business Units**, each responsible for a subsystem or process area. As Project Manager, I:
- Led weekly stand-ups and design reviews
- Coordinated interfaces between Business Units
- Managed requirement changes and risk mitigation
- Maintained schedules and ensured on-time delivery
- Oversaw shared documentation and configuration control

This structure closely mirrored a real-world manufacturing organization and required constant cross-functional coordination.

---

## System Design & Business Unit Contributions

### BU01 – Clearance Mount
- Primary locating feature for the PCB
- Utilized cantilever clips for strength and repeatability
- Designed with printer tolerances and material behavior in mind
- Emphasized low-cost, manufacturable geometry

### BU02 & BU03 – Standoff Mounts
- Provided rigid interfaces between the PCB and clearance mount
- Manual assembly revealed shallow dowel pin engagement
- Design feedback captured and documented for iteration

### BU04 – Sheet Metal Clips
- Prevented motion in the Z-direction
- Secured PCB and display mounting plate
- Evaluated tradeoffs between retention strength and serviceability

### BU05 – Fixture Design
- Designed fixtures to support repeatable and consistent assembly
- Considered ergonomic access and alignment during installation
- Supported downstream assembly and inspection processes

### BU06 – Robotic Assembly
- Evaluated feasibility of robotic handling and alignment
- Considered automation constraints and part orientation
- Supported long-term scalability of the assembly process

### BU07 – Manual Assembly
- Developed step-by-step manual assembly procedures
- Evaluated assembly time, ergonomics, and error risk
- Identified opportunities to simplify part handling and sequencing
- Provided feedback to design teams to improve assembly robustness

### BU08 – Inspection & Quality
- Defined inspection criteria and measurement methods
- Conducted dimensional inspections of manufactured components
- Analyzed results against control limits
- Identified opportunities to recenter processes for improved yield
